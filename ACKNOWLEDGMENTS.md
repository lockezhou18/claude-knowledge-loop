# Acknowledgments

These skills build on ideas spanning 2,400 years of systematic inquiry and cutting-edge AI research. Credit where it's due.

## Philosophy

The `/research` skill's inquiry cycle draws from three traditions:

- **Socratic Method** — The elenchus process (state belief → question → expose contradiction → refine). The 6 types of Socratic questions come from the [University of Michigan CRLT](https://crlt.umich.edu/).
- **Aristotle's Four Causes** (*Metaphysics*, ~350 BCE) — Material, Formal, Efficient, Final causes as a framework for complete understanding.
- **C.S. Peirce** — Abductive reasoning as the mechanism for generating hypotheses from surprising observations.

Also influenced by:
- **Karl Popper**, *The Logic of Scientific Discovery* (1959) — Falsifiability as the test of useful hypotheses
- **Thomas Kuhn**, *The Structure of Scientific Revolutions* (1962) — The role of anomalies and paradigm shifts

## Knowledge Management

The `/compound` and `/compound-refresh` skills implement ideas from:

- **Niklas Luhmann's Zettelkasten** — Atomic notes with bidirectional links. The aggregation check (3+ notes → synthesize a pattern) is how episodic knowledge becomes semantic knowledge.
- **Mem0** ([github.com/mem0ai/mem0](https://github.com/mem0ai/mem0)) — Cross-agent shared memory. 26% accuracy improvement and 90% token savings from structured memory.
- **A-Mem** (NeurIPS 2025) — Zettelkasten-style agent memory with automatic linking and retrieval.
- **Reflexion** (Shinn et al., 2023; [arXiv:2303.11366](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.11366)) — Verbal self-reflection as episodic memory.

## Engineering Pipeline

The `/lfg` pipeline's gated approach draws from:

- **Constitutional AI** (Anthropic, 2022; [arXiv:2212.08073](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.08073)) — AI systems that govern themselves through explicit principles.
- **IterAlign** (Xiong et al., NAACL 2024; [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/2024.naacl-long.78/)) — Constitutional self-evolution through auto-red-teaming.
- **Self-Refine** (Madaan et al., 2023; [arXiv:2303.17651](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.17651)) — The generate/feedback/refine loop.
- **MetaClaw** (2026; [arXiv:2603.17187](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17187)) — Zero-cost self-evolution via skill synthesis.
